Who We Are

Small studio.
Unlimited imagination.

Arcadium Labs is an independent game development studio focused on building accessible and engaging digital gaming experiences. We believe great games can turn a few spare minutes into moments of discovery, challenge and enjoyment.

Our growing portfolio spans mobile gaming experiences, with a focus on bringing fresh ideas to players and continually exploring what we can create next.

Whether you enjoy solving problems, testing your reflexes or discovering something new, our goal is to make finding your next game simple.
