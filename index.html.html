Privacy Policy

How Arcadium Labs approaches personal information and privacy across its website and mobile games.

This policy is intended to explain the types of information that may be processed through Arcadium Labs' website and mobile games, why that information may be needed, and the choices available to users.

Different games may use different technologies and services. The information actually collected depends on the particular game, its configuration and the services enabled in it.

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Arcadium Labs website and the mobile games or related services to which this policy is linked. Individual games may provide additional privacy disclosures where their features or third-party services require them.

2. Information that may be processed

Depending on the website or game you use, information that may be processed includes:

  • Technical information: device type, operating system, app version, language, and diagnostic information needed to troubleshoot technical issues.
  • Usage information: game interactions, gameplay progress, performance events and crash reports, where these features or services are enabled.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers, ad interactions or related information processed by advertising providers, where advertising services are used.
  • Messages and support information: information you choose to provide when contacting us or submitting a support request.
  • Purchase information: transaction-related information made available through the relevant app store or payment provider. Payment card details are handled according to that provider's systems and policies.

This list describes categories that may apply. It is not a claim that every Arcadium Labs game collects every type of information listed above.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Provide and operate the website and mobile games.
  • Maintain game functionality and remember game progress where supported.
  • Identify crashes, errors, bugs and performance problems.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.
  • Process or help resolve purchase-related enquiries.
  • Display and measure advertisements where advertising is enabled.
  • Protect services against fraud, abuse and technical threats.
  • Meet applicable legal and platform requirements.

4. Advertising and third-party services

Some games may use third-party providers for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, app distribution or other functionality. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and the settings enabled in the relevant game.

If Google AdMob or another advertising service is integrated into a particular game, that provider may process device information, advertising identifiers, ad interaction information and other data as described in its disclosures. Personalised advertising and tracking behaviour may depend on applicable law, consent requirements, device settings and provider configuration.

Google Play also processes information in connection with distributing apps and operating its services. Review the relevant provider's privacy information for details.

Google privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Cookies and website technologies

The website may use essential technical features to display content and operate correctly. Third-party resources, such as externally hosted fonts, may result in requests to their providers when a page loads.

Any analytics, advertising cookies, tracking pixels or similar technologies must be disclosed and managed according to the technologies actually deployed and the legal requirements that apply. Where consent is required, it should be obtained before the relevant non-essential technologies are activated.

6. Data storage and retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, maintain security, resolve support requests, meet legal obligations or address disputes. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the service processing it.

Some games may store gameplay progress or settings locally on a device. Other games may use platform or third-party services. The actual storage method varies by game.

7. Sharing of information

Personal information is not intended to be sold by Arcadium Labs. Information may be shared where necessary with service providers operating relevant features, app distribution platforms, professional advisers, or competent authorities where legally required.

Any such sharing should be limited to an appropriate purpose and governed by applicable legal, contractual and privacy requirements.

8. Children's privacy

Children's privacy is important. The applicable age requirements, content ratings, advertising configuration and parental-consent obligations depend on each game's intended audience, features and distribution regions.

Games intended for children must use appropriate safeguards and comply with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements where relevant. Do not submit personal information through a game or website unless it is appropriate and authorised to do so.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures should be used to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No website, application, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on your location and the laws that apply, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restrictions on the processing of personal information. You may also have rights to object to certain processing or withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis.

You can also review available device-level advertising privacy controls and the settings offered by relevant third-party providers.

Requests may be submitted through the support contact published on the relevant Google Play app listing. Please identify the relevant game and describe your request without including passwords, payment card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

11. International processing

Some service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for international transfers in accordance with applicable data protection law.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when our games, services, technologies or legal obligations change. The updated version should be published here with a revised effective date. Material changes may require additional notice where applicable.
