Legal, Store & Platform Requirements

Privacy & Data Protection

Arcadium Labs is committed to responsible handling of user information in alignment with global privacy standards including POPIA and GDPR principles.

Advertising Compliance

All monetization systems are designed to align with Google AdMob, Google Play Developer Policies, and fair engagement standards.

Child Safety

Arcadium Labs does not support exploitative advertising, unsafe user interactions, or prohibited child-directed monetization practices.

Intellectual Property

All games, branding, assets, source code, audio, and designs remain protected intellectual property of Arcadium Labs.

Store Disclosures

Game listings should include accurate screenshots, age ratings, privacy labels, ad disclosures, in-app purchase disclosures where applicable, and support contact details.

Accessibility

The studio should aim for readable contrast, responsive layouts, accessible labels, keyboard-friendly navigation, and clear support pathways.

Security

Official links, app store buttons, support emails, privacy pages, and future analytics integrations should be reviewed to reduce phishing, spoofing, and user trust risks.

Privacy Policy

Arcadium Labs may collect limited technical, gameplay, analytics, advertising, and support-related information to improve game functionality, security, performance, and player experience. User information is handled according to applicable privacy regulations including POPIA and GDPR principles.


Data collected may include device identifiers, gameplay progression, crash logs, advertising interaction metrics, and support communication details. Arcadium Labs does not intentionally sell personal user information.

Terms of Service

By using Arcadium Labs websites, games, and services, users agree to follow platform rules, applicable laws, fair gameplay expectations, and monetization policies. Abuse, cheating, exploitation, automated farming, reverse engineering, or malicious activity may result in account or service restrictions.

Cookie Policy

Arcadium Labs websites may use cookies, analytics technologies, local storage systems, and advertising tools to improve functionality, remember preferences, measure traffic, and optimize performance.

Community Guidelines

Players and community members are expected to maintain respectful behaviour across all Arcadium Labs platforms. Harassment, hate speech, scams, exploit abuse, impersonation, cheating, and harmful conduct are prohibited.

Refund Policy

Purchases made through Google Play or Apple App Store platforms are generally managed according to the refund policies of the respective platform providers. Arcadium Labs may review support requests relating to technical purchase issues or accidental transactions.

DMCA & Copyright Policy

All Arcadium Labs games, branding, source code, visual assets, audio assets, trademarks, and related materials are protected intellectual property. Unauthorized copying, redistribution, asset extraction, cloning, or resale is prohibited.


Copyright concerns, infringement notices, or intellectual property disputes may be submitted through the official Arcadium Labs support channels.
