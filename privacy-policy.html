Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

Arcadium Labs develops and publishes mobile games and interactive entertainment experiences. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled across all Arcadium Labs games, applications, websites, and related services.

Information We Collect

Arcadium Labs does not require users to create accounts for most of its games and does not directly collect personal information through gameplay.

Game progress, settings, high scores, unlocked content, and preferences may be stored locally on the user's device to support gameplay functionality and improve the user experience.

Advertising Services

Some Arcadium Labs games display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may automatically collect certain information including:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP addresses
  • Diagnostic information
  • Ad interaction data

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Storage

Where applicable, game progress and settings are stored locally on the user's device. Arcadium Labs does not maintain centralized databases containing player profiles for games that do not provide account-based functionality.

Third-Party Services

Arcadium Labs games may utilize one or more of the following services:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Android Operating System Services

Children's Privacy

Arcadium Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where required, our games are designed to comply with Google Play Families Policies and applicable child protection regulations.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the integrity of our applications and services. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

International Users

Arcadium Labs products may be distributed globally through digital marketplaces. Third-party providers may process information in different countries according to their own privacy practices and legal obligations.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights related to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and objection to certain data uses.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Arcadium Labs may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes. Updates will be published on this page together with the revised effective date.
