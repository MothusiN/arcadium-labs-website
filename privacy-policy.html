Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Arcadium Labs approaches personal information in connection with its website and mobile games.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Publisher review required: Before publishing, confirm the actual data collected by each game, all third-party SDKs, advertising and analytics services, the privacy contact address and the disclosures in Google Play's Data safety section. Remove or amend any statement that does not match the actual implementation.

1. Who We Are

“Arcadium Labs”, “we”, “us” or “our” refers to the publisher responsible for the Arcadium Labs website and mobile games. Our published games can be found on our official Google Play developer page .

2. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to the Arcadium Labs website and, where applicable, our mobile games. Individual games may use different features, permissions and third-party services. Actual processing depends on the game version, its configuration, the device and the services enabled. Game-specific disclosures should be checked against the relevant store listing and in-app notices.

3. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on the service and features enabled, information may include:

  • Information you provide: information included in a support email or privacy request, such as your reply address and description of the issue.
  • Technical information: device model, operating-system version, app version, performance information and crash diagnostics, if collected by the game or its service providers.
  • Gameplay information: progress, settings or gameplay events where a feature or service stores or processes that information.
  • Advertising and analytics information: ad impressions, interactions, advertising identifiers or usage measurements if an advertising or analytics SDK is enabled.
  • Website technical information: server logs and request information that may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver, maintain and protect the site.

This list does not mean every category is collected by every game. The actual data practices must be verified for each published release.

4. How Information May Be Used

Where applicable, information may be used to provide game and website functionality, maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose technical issues, respond to support requests, remember settings, measure performance, display or measure advertisements and comply with legal obligations.

5. Advertising, Analytics and Third-Party Services

Our games may integrate third-party services, such as Google Play services, advertising providers, analytics tools or crash-reporting SDKs. Those services may process information under their own privacy terms and configurations. The services used, data categories, purposes and available controls should be disclosed accurately in the relevant game listing and in-app notices.

For information about Google's practices, visit Google's Privacy Policy .

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website code provided with this policy does not intentionally set its own tracking cookies or use browser local storage. Hosting infrastructure or third-party resources may process technical information independently. If analytics, advertising pixels or non-essential cookies are added, this policy and any required consent controls should be updated.

7. Sharing and Disclosure

Where applicable, information may be processed by service providers acting on our behalf, shared when you request a service, disclosed where required by law or used to protect users and our services. We do not offer personal information for sale. This statement does not override processing carried out by third parties under their own terms.

8. Retention and Security

We aim to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose, a support case, a security requirement or a legal obligation. Information should then be deleted or de-identified where appropriate. We use reasonable safeguards suitable for the service, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Children and Family Audiences

Age suitability and child-directed requirements vary by game. We aim to handle children's information responsibly and configure advertising, permissions and SDKs in accordance with applicable law and platform policies. Parents or guardians with concerns should use the support contact provided on the relevant Google Play listing. Each game's target audience and child-safety requirements must be reviewed before release.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; withdraw consent; or complain to a relevant regulator. We may need to verify your identity and may be unable to fulfil requests where a lawful exception applies.

11. South Africa and International Visitors

Where applicable, we aim to handle personal information consistently with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and other applicable privacy laws. Applicable rights and obligations depend on the circumstances and services involved. This policy is not a certification of legal compliance.

12. International Processing

Hosting and third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for international transfers. The providers and transfer arrangements used by each service should be verified.

13. Links to Other Services

Our website links to Google Play and may link to other third-party services. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. Review the relevant provider's policy before submitting information to it.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our website, games, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date. Material changes should be communicated where required by law or platform policy.

15. Contact and Privacy Requests

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact shown on the relevant Arcadium Labs Google Play listing . Before publication, confirm that this contact is monitored and able to receive and handle privacy requests. If necessary, add a dedicated privacy email address here.
