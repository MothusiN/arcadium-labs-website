Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how Arcadium Labs may collect, use, disclose and protect information when you visit our website or use our mobile games and related services.

Effective date: 12 October 2026

1. Introduction

Arcadium Labs ("we", "us" or "our") develops and publishes mobile games and related digital experiences. We respect privacy and aim to handle personal information responsibly.

This policy applies to the Arcadium Labs website, our mobile games and related services that link to or reference this policy. Individual games or third-party services may have additional privacy notices that apply to their specific features.

By using our website or games, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this policy. Where consent is legally required, we will seek it as appropriate.

2. Information We May Collect

The information handled depends on the game, features enabled, device settings and services integrated into that game. Depending on the circumstances, information may include:

  • Technical and device information: device model, operating system, app version, language, general device settings, IP address and diagnostic information.
  • Usage and gameplay information: game progress, session duration, interactions, feature usage, crash reports and performance information.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers, ad impressions, interactions, frequency and information used by advertising partners to deliver or measure advertisements.
  • Support communications: information you choose to provide when contacting us, such as your email address, message and relevant troubleshooting details.
  • Purchase information: transaction status or purchase-related records made available through a platform or service provider. Payment card details are generally handled by the relevant payment platform rather than directly by us.

We do not intend to collect more information than is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. The categories above describe information that may be processed; they do not mean every game collects every category.

3. Information Collected Automatically

Our website hosting provider, app distribution platform, analytics tools or other integrated services may automatically process certain technical information when you access a website or use a game. This may be necessary to deliver content, maintain security, understand technical failures or support service operation.

Whether analytics, diagnostic tools or other collection technologies are used depends on the specific website or game implementation.

4. How We Use Information

Where applicable and legally permitted, information may be used to:

  • Provide, operate and maintain our website and games.
  • Save or manage game progress where the relevant game supports it.
  • Identify crashes, fix bugs and improve performance and usability.
  • Display and measure advertising, including relevant advertising where enabled and permitted.
  • Process or validate transactions through relevant platforms.
  • Respond to support requests and user feedback.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, security threats and technical misuse.
  • Meet legal obligations and protect our rights and services.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we may rely on one or more of the following: your consent, the performance of a contract or provision of a service you request, compliance with legal obligations, or legitimate interests such as maintaining security and improving service reliability. The appropriate basis depends on the information and activity involved.

6. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Some games may use third-party advertising, app distribution, analytics, crash reporting, cloud or other service providers. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and contractual arrangements.

Third-party advertising services may use device identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, limit, personalise or measure ads, depending on the services integrated, your settings and applicable law. Where required, consent will be requested before relevant processing.

Our games may be distributed through Google Play. Platform features, account settings and transactions may be governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy notices.

We do not control the privacy practices of independent third-party services. Please review their policies to understand how they handle information.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or third-party services may use cookies, local storage, SDKs or similar technologies where applicable. These technologies may be used for essential website operation, security, preferences, analytics or advertising.

The technologies used depend on the implementation of the relevant website or game. You may be able to manage cookies through your browser and advertising preferences through your device or applicable service settings. Disabling certain technologies may affect some features.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties in exchange for money. Depending on the service and applicable law, information may be shared with:

  • Service providers that support hosting, technical operations, analytics, diagnostics, advertising, security or customer support.
  • Distribution and payment platforms that provide app distribution, billing or related services.
  • Professional advisers where necessary for legitimate business, legal or compliance purposes.
  • Authorities or other parties where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, users or services.
  • Business successors in connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring or transfer of relevant business assets, subject to applicable law.

We aim to limit sharing to information reasonably needed for the relevant purpose and to use appropriate safeguards where required.

9. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including service operation, security, dispute resolution and legal compliance. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the service provider involved.

Where information is no longer required, we aim to delete it, anonymise it or otherwise handle it in accordance with applicable retention requirements.

10. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved. However, no website, application, network or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should keep their devices and applications updated and use appropriate device security settings.

11. Children's Privacy

We recognise the importance of protecting children's privacy. Our games and services should be assessed individually to determine their intended audience and any applicable child-protection requirements.

Where a game is directed at children or is subject to specific child-privacy rules, we must apply the relevant legal requirements, including any required parental consent, restrictions on data collection and advertising safeguards.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

12. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to request access to personal information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability or to object to certain processing. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

You may have additional rights under laws applicable to your jurisdiction, including South African data protection law where relevant. These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the contact route provided at the end of this policy. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the request before responding.

You can also manage certain advertising or app permissions through your device settings and applicable platform controls.

13. International Processing

Some service providers may process information in countries other than the country in which you live. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, appropriate measures should be implemented for the relevant transfer.

14. External Links

Our website and games may link to third-party websites, app stores or services. We are not responsible for the content, security or privacy practices of those external services. Review their respective policies before providing information.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our games, technology, service providers or legal obligations. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or seek consent for material changes.

16. Contact Arcadium Labs

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, or concerns about information associated with our games, please contact Arcadium Labs through the official developer contact details available on our Google Play developer page.
